## 3. Full Description

### English

**The real Argentine Truco — bluffing included.**

Monolito is the classic Río de la Plata card game, complete and faithful: full envido chains (Envido, Envido-Envido, Real Envido, Falta Envido), truco escalation (Truco → Retruco → Vale Cuatro), ir al mazo, every parda tie-break case, and an optional Flor variant with Contraflor and Contraflor al resto. First to 30 points, malas and buenas, mano wins ties — the rules were verified against independent published sources.

**Play it your way:**
- **Solo vs El Monolito** — a cunning AI opponent that bluffs, sings envido, and knows when to fold. Works fully offline.
- **Pass & play** — two players, one device, phone passed across the table.
- **Online 1v1** — open a private table and share a short table code with a friend.
- **Online 2v2** — team truco, partners across the table. Share one code with up to three friends, and fill any empty seat with a bot.

**Built for real matches:**
- Dropped connection? Rejoin with the same table code and the hand picks up exactly where it left off — in 2v2 a bot quietly holds your seat until you're back.
- Table chat in the lobby and mid-game.
- Rename yourself anytime.

**A deck worth looking at:** all 40 cards of the baraja española, hand-drawn in SVG with authentic Castilian-pattern details — the pinta border-breaks and traditional pip layouts — restyled in navy and gold on a midnight holotable.

Full English and Spanish interface. Free. No ads, no accounts, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Online play is peer-to-peer — nothing is stored on any server.

¡Quiero vale cuatro!

## 6. Privacy Questionnaire (App Privacy in App Store Connect)

**Recommended answer: "Data Not Collected."**

Rationale / how to answer:
- The app has **no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs that phone home, and no server-side storage**. Nothing is collected off-device.
- Online play uses **PeerJS over WebRTC**. Display names and chat messages travel **peer-to-peer between players' devices** and are **ephemeral** — never stored on a server, never linked to identity, never used for tracking. Under Apple's definition, "collected" means transmitted off-device *and stored/accessible by you or partners* — this data is not, so it does not count as collected.
- One caveat to be aware of (not a questionnaire answer): connecting to online play contacts the **public PeerJS signaling broker** (0.peerjs.com) to exchange connection info, and WebRTC exchanges IP addresses between peers to establish the direct connection. This is standard connection plumbing, not data collection by you. If a reviewer asks, describe it exactly that way.
- Answer **No** to: tracking, third-party advertising, data linked to identity, data used for tracking.
- Privacy Nutrition Label result: **"Data Not Collected."**
- You'll also need a **privacy policy URL** (required even for Data Not Collected). A one-paragraph page on GitHub Pages stating the above is enough.

## 7. Age Rating Questionnaire

Expected result: **4+** (or 12+ only if you flag frequent mild language — you shouldn't need to).

- Cartoon/fantasy violence: **None**
- Realistic violence: **None**
- Sexual content / nudity: **None**
- Profanity or crude humor: **None** — the AI's taunts are mild, playful trash talk with no profanity. If in doubt about a specific taunt string, "Infrequent/Mild" profanity → 9+, but current content shouldn't require it.
- Horror/fear themes: **None**
- **Simulated gambling: NO** — Truco is a trick-taking card game with **no wagering, no chips, no currency, no betting of any kind**. "Points" are game score only. Using playing cards alone does not make a game simulated gambling.
- Contests: **None**
- Unrestricted web access: **No**
- User-generated content / communication: the app has **peer-to-peer table chat** between people who exchanged a table code privately. Apple's age questionnaire doesn't ask about this directly, but be ready to mention it in Review Notes: chat only works between players who share a private code, is ephemeral, and is not stored.
- Medical, drugs, alcohol/tobacco: **None**
