# Argentine Truco: The Definitive Rules

**Truco** is the ultimate Argentine game of strategy, hierarchy, and "mentira" (bluffing). It is played with a 40-card Spanish deck (removing the 8s and 9s) by 2, 4, or 6 players.

---

## 1. Hierarchy of Cards (High to Low)
In Truco, numerical value is secondary to a card's "rank." Cards are divided into **Bravas** (Strong) and **Falsas** (Weak).

| Rank | Card Name | Spanish Term |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Ace of Swords** | *Ancho de Espada* |
| **2** | **Ace of Clubs** | *Ancho de Basto* |
| **3** | **7 of Swords** | *Siete de Espada* |
| **4** | **7 of Gold** | *Siete de Oro* |
| **5** | **All 3s** | *Los Treses* |
| **6** | **All 2s** | *Los Doses* |
| **7** | **False Aces** (Cup/Gold) | *Anchos Falsos* |
| **8** | **Kings** (12s) | *Los Reyes* |
| **9** | **Knights** (11s) | *Los Caballos* |
| **10** | **Jacks** (10s) | *Las Sotas* |
| **11** | **False 7s** (Cup/Club) | *Sietes Falsos* |
| **12-14** | **6s, 5s, 4s** | *Las Bajas* |

## 2. Gameplay & "La Parda" (Ties)
A hand consists of three tricks. The team that wins 2 out of 3 tricks wins the point(s) for the **Truco** phase.

### Solving Ties (*Emparde*)
* **Tie in 1st:** Whoever wins the 2nd trick wins the hand. If the 2nd also ties, the 3rd decides.
* **Tie in 2nd:** Whoever won the 1st trick wins the hand.
* **Tie in 3rd:** Whoever won the 1st trick wins the hand.
* **Triple Tie:** If all three tricks are tied, the **Mano** (the player who started the hand) wins.

---

## 3. The Betting Phases
Bets must be called in order: **Flor** (if used) > **Envido** > **Truco**.

### El Envido (The Points)
This is a comparison of two cards of the same suit, called during the first trick.
* **Calculation:** (Sum of two same-suit cards) + 20. 
* **Face Card Value:** 10s, 11s, and 12s are worth **0 points** for the sum.
* **The "Falta Envido":** * If you are in the first 15 points (**Malas**), the winner takes the whole match.
    * If you are in the second 15 points (**Buenas**), the winner gets the points the leader needs to win.

### El Truco (The Tricks)
This raises the stakes of the 2-out-of-3 trick game.
* **Truco:** 2 points.
* **Retruco:** 3 points.
* **Vale Cuatro:** 4 points.
* *Note:* If a player calls "Truco" and the opponent says "No quiero," the challenger wins 1 point immediately.

---

## 4. Etiquette & Strategy
* **Mano vs. Pie:** The *Mano* starts the play. The *Pie* (Foot) is the last player on a team and acts as the captain.
* **Ir al Mazo:** If your cards are terrible, you can "go to the deck," forfeiting the current hand value to the opponents.
* **The Bluff:** You may lie about your Envido score until forced to show your cards at the end. You **must** show your cards if you win a bet to prove you weren't "lying" about the math.
