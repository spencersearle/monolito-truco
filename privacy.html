Privacy Policy & Terms of Use — last updated 2026-07-21

The short version

Monolito does not collect, store, sell, or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. There is no game server holding your information, because there is no game server at all.

What stays on your device

The app saves a few preferences locally, in your browser or app storage. This information never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage:

  • Your chosen display name
  • Language, sound, vibration, and appearance settings
  • Your solo win/loss record
  • Players you have blocked, and any reports you have filed

Online play

Online games connect players' devices directly to each other using WebRTC (peer-to-peer). Card play and chat messages travel between the players in a game and are never stored anywhere.

To let two devices find each other, the app uses the free public PeerJS signaling broker. That service briefly sees a randomly generated table code and the network address needed to open the connection. It does not see your name, your chat, or your game. Once the players are connected, the broker is no longer involved.

Your display name is visible to the other players at your table for the duration of the game. Chat messages exist only in the open app and are gone when the game ends.

Children

Monolito is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Terms of use — table talk

Online tables include a chat, which means you may encounter messages written by other players. By playing online you agree that:

  • There is no tolerance for abusive, harassing, hateful, or otherwise objectionable content or behavior.
  • You will not use the chat to post such content, to impersonate another person, or to harass other players.
  • Players who violate this may be blocked by others and may lose access to online play.

How we handle objectionable content

  • Automatic filtering. Strong language and slurs are masked in chat automatically, in both English and Spanish, on the sending and receiving side.
  • Report. Tap any message in the chat to report it. The report is recorded and the sender is blocked immediately.
  • Block. Tap any message to block its sender. Their messages disappear at once — including ones already on screen — and stay hidden in every future game. Blocked players can be cleared any time in Settings.
  • Contact. Reports of abuse sent to the address below are reviewed and acted on within 24 hours.
